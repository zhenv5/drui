# Mini-game scene design and verification

Use a screen map: HUD (secondary), playfield (primary), control/answer zone (reachable), pause/help (discoverable), feedback overlay (transient). Test at least default, help, correct, incorrect, partial progress, pause/resume, completion and no-input/invalid input. For drag/line play test pointer ownership, cancel, snapping, accidental tap, overlap, safe area and low frame rate. Keep maths and state in game engine, not art assets. Feel: immediate low-latency highlight, brief easing/squash, audio/haptic if enabled, celebration sized to achievement; support Reduce Motion. Do not sacrifice legibility to effects. Record game logic regression, simulator/device captures, and a successful real task end to end. Effects like light trails/dot patterns/flowing gradients must have an explicit design reason, fallbacks and a frame budget.
